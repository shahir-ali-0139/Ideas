🌵 OBSTACLES & SCORING

  • Cacti: Small, large, and clustered. Leap over them cleanly!
  • Flying Pterodactyls (Birds): Appear at 3 distinct heights:
    • Low Birds: Jump over them!
    • Mid Birds: Duck and slide underneath!
    • High Birds: Fly harmlessly overhead!
  • Cyber Drones & Laser Obstacles: Watch for glowing hazards in advanced levels!
  • Close Call Bonus: Clear an obstacle right at the skin of your scales to score an instant +100 Bonus Points!
