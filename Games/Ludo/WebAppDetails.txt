4. GAMEPLAY AND RULES

Implement complete Ludo gameplay.

Essential mechanics:

Players must roll a 6 to bring a token out of the home cage.
Each player gets four tokens.
Players move according to the dice value.
Allow token selection when multiple tokens can move.
Capture opponent tokens when landing on eligible squares.
Captured tokens return to their respective home cages.
Safe squares protect tokens.
Rolling a 6 grants another turn according to the configured rules.
Tokens must follow the correct path.
Tokens must reach their exact finishing position.
A token cannot overshoot the final destination.
Detect when a player has finished all four tokens.
Display the winner.
Support a new game and replay option.

Implement a centralized rules engine so all game modes use the same gameplay logic.
