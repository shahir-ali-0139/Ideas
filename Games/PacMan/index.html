HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP CONTROLS

▲ / W Move Up

▼ / S Move Down

◀ / A Move Left

▶ / D Move Right

P or ESC Pause / Resume

M Mute Background Music

MOBILE & TABLET

• Tap on-screen D-Pad arrow buttons

• Or swipe in any direction directly across the maze

• Corner buffering lets you turn smoothly without getting stuck!

MULTI-DIRECTIONAL WARP TUNNELS

• 3 Horizontal Tunnels: Upper, Middle & Lower side tunnels warp left ◀▶ right

• 2 Vertical Tunnels: Warp top ▲▼ bottom across columns 6 & 21

• Look for glowing cyan exit arrows (◀ ▶ ▲ ▼) at maze borders

• Ghosts slow down inside tunnels—use them to escape!

SCORING & RULES

• Pellets = 10 pts | Energizers = 50 pts

• Eat Energizers to make ghosts vulnerable

• Eat ghosts for 200, 400, 800, 1600 pts!

• Bonus Fruit spawns under ghost house for extra points

• Extra Life awarded at 10,000 points!
